# Mars Colony — 58 s cut (archived)

This is the first **Mars Colony: Life Support Failed** Short, rendered at 57.9 s with the
`choice` preset. It is kept for reference, not used by the live catalogue.

Why it was replaced:

- Four named characters (Voss, Chen, Kovic) and four plans were introduced in one breath.
- The "one power cell, one system" rule arrived after the options, so the choice never read
  as a choice.
- The options were mood images ("red dust", "stars forever") instead of action + power cost
  + consequence.
- The viewer was asked to vote before the film had earned the question.

The replacement is the 90 s `choice-long` cut: Wren's POV, one rule stated early, four
options with a cost each, Wren chooses C, then the vote judges her.

Files:

- `episode/` — the old `src/episodes/mars-colony-survival/` data (`episode.json`, `shots.json`,
  `index.tsx`, `timeline.json`, `words.json`).
- `out/` — the old final master, raw render, chunks, QA stills, report and publish kit.
- `public/` — the old voice, SFX and music stems.

The live episode is `shorts-studio/src/episodes/mars-colony-survival/` at 90 s.
